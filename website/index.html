Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 2026

Red Carpet Player is designed around local-first storage. This policy explains exactly what stays on your device and what, if anything, ever leaves it.

On-Device Storage Only

  • All playlist URLs, usernames, passwords, and provider API credentials you enter are stored only in your device's local storage (localStorage / IndexedDB).
  • Your cached channel list, guide data, and search index are built and kept entirely on-device.
  • None of the above is transmitted to, or stored on, any server operated by us.

What the app contacts on your behalf

  • The player connects directly from your device to the playlist or network tuner endpoint you provide, and to any optional third-party XMLTV/EPG address you choose to add in Settings — never through a server we control.
  • Optional public fixture-schedule lookups (used only to label events in the guide) are read-only and contain no personal data.

Pro License Checkout & Activation

  • If you purchase Red Carpet Pro, payment is handled entirely by an external, PCI-compliant Merchant of Record — we never see or store your card details. That processor collects your billing email and payment details to issue a license key.
  • The app itself only ever sees and verifies that pseudonymous license key, locally, to unlock Pro features. It is not linked to any profile or account on our side.

What we don't do

  • We don't operate a central account system, analytics pipeline, or ad network inside the app.
